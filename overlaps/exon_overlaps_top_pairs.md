## Notes on the kinds of overlap

- Clusters with alternative first exons on shared constant exons: Pcdha/Pcdhg (436 of the
  pairs) and Ugt1a (all 36 pairs among its 9 genes). Only the first exon distinguishes members;
  5'-truncated reads, common with ONT, cover only the shared exons and fit every member equally.
  Candidates for treating each cluster as one unit, or assigning by first exon.
- Second gene models over most of a gene: Srcap / Gm42715 (28 shared introns), Cmtr1 / Gm28043
  (20), Ctnnd1 / Gm28635 (18), Mapkapk5 / Gm42878 (12). In Ensembl 116 these are separate gene
  models (CCDS and/or `Ensembl_canonical` on most), not tagged `readthrough_transcript`, so a
  tag filter won't remove them. Reads over the shared exons fit both; the assignment rests on
  the terminal exons. The main risk for coding-gene DTU.
- Nested or bicistronic loci: Zbed6 / Zc3h11a (all 16 Zbed6 introns shared; Zbed6 sits in a
  Zc3h11a intron, so presumably retained-intron Zc3h11a transcripts), Rpp14 / Htd2 (3; reported
  as bicistronic), Tymp / Sco2 (2). Reads moving between these genes may be real biology rather
  than assignment noise.
- One locus under two gene IDs with the same name (e.g. Pakap, Ptp4a1): 102 of the pairs.
  Merging by gene name would remove these.
- Small-RNA genes inside host exons (snoRNA, miRNA etc.) share no introns with their hosts, so
  they drop out here; with exon overlap alone they were 1,009 of the same-strand pairs.
- Other / other is mostly lncRNA with transcribed unprocessed pseudogenes (379 pairs).
